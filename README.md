# Toybird Apps Site

GitHub Pages website for Toybird Apps.

## Purpose

Toybird Apps publishes useful utilities designed to make everyday work and daily life clearer, smoother, and easier.

## Manual Upload

1. Download and extract the ZIP package.
2. Open the extracted `toybird-apps-site_english_polished` folder.
3. Upload the contents of that folder to the root of the `toybird-apps-site` GitHub repository.
4. Commit the changes.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages deployment to finish.
6. Open the URLs above and verify that the pages load correctly.

## Verification Checklist

After publishing, verify:

- The top page shows both Pointer Cue and Meet Here Card.
- Pointer Cue pages still load correctly.
- Meet Here Card English and Japanese product pages load correctly.
- Meet Here Card English and Japanese support pages load correctly.
- Meet Here Card English and Japanese privacy policy pages load correctly.
- Language-switch links work.
- The `support@toybird.com` mail link opens correctly.
