Website services

This section covers labs.toybird.com and lp.toybird.com. Updated October 26, 2026.

The portfolio and product guides at lp.toybird.com use Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions, such as language selection and links to product stores. Google may process browser and device information and use cookies under its Privacy Policy.

Language-switching pages can save your chosen language in browser local storage and use session storage for a language-selection event. You can clear this information through your browser’s site-data settings.

Some pages include YouTube videos or remotely hosted images and badges. Loading or playing that content can contact its provider. Links to stores, social platforms, and other websites open services with their own privacy practices.

The sites are hosted with GitHub Pages. Hosting requests are subject to the provider’s privacy statement.
