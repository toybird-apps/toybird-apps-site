AI Study Sheet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Operator and scope

Toybird Labs develops and provides AI Study Sheet (Japanese name: AI赤シート). This policy applies to the AI Study Sheet app for iPhone and iPad.

Information processed on your device

Material photos, images, PDFs, OCR text, extracted terms, masks, page titles and notes, folders, settings, and study or review history are processed and stored locally. To resume imports, the app may keep a copy of a selected PDF, processing state, drafts, and import history in its local storage.

Study activity used for review recommendations

Your three-step self-ratings, study dates and activity, and review timing are processed and saved locally to select future review pages. The app does not automatically grade answers. Backups you choose to export include study history; shared study materials do not transfer your personal study history.

External servers

Study materials, including photos, images, PDFs, recognized text, extracted terms, and masks, are processed locally on your device. They are not uploaded to external AI services, Studyplus, or Toybird Labs servers. If you choose to use Studyplus integration, only the study record information described below is transmitted to Studyplus. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and Toybird Labs does not collect study materials through the app. AI Study Sheet itself does not require an account; Studyplus integration requires a Studyplus account.

Optional Studyplus integration

Studyplus integration is optional, and automatic recording is off by default. After you confirm your Studyplus visibility settings, expressly consent, and authorize the connection in Studyplus, newly started study sessions can be recorded automatically when they end. You can also submit a record manually.

Each Studyplus record contains the confirmed study duration in whole seconds, the study session end date and time, and the fixed comment “AIStudySheetで学習”. The official Studyplus SDK uses an authentication token to associate the record with your Studyplus account. Materials, OCR text, extracted terms, page titles, notes, masks, self-ratings, and detailed review history are not included. Sessions started before automatic recording was enabled and earlier unsent records are not automatically posted. Failed or uncertain posts are not automatically retried.

The visibility and handling of posted records follow your Studyplus settings and the Studyplus Privacy Policy. AI Study Sheet cannot read or change your Studyplus visibility settings. You can turn off automatic recording or disconnect from the app's settings at any time. These actions do not cancel a request already in progress, revoke authorization on Studyplus's side, or delete records already posted to Studyplus.

Purchases

Unlimited Saves purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment; Toybird Labs does not receive payment-card numbers. The app may contact Apple services to verify purchase status.

Camera, photos, and files

Device features are used to capture materials and import the photos, PDFs, or backups you select. The OS requests permission when required. Manage permissions in device Settings.

Backups and sharing

You can export backups containing materials and study history or share masked images. Studyplus delivery history and authentication tokens are not included in backups exported from AI Study Sheet. If you choose iCloud Drive or another storage or sharing service, that provider's policy applies. The app does not automatically synchronize materials to a Toybird Labs cloud service.

Review notifications

Optional review reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Disable them in the app or through OS notification settings.

Managing stored data

Manage or delete saved materials and drafts in the app. Studyplus consent settings and delivery history are stored locally, and the official Studyplus SDK stores its authentication token in the device's Keychain. The local delivery journal is excluded from device backups. Turning off automatic recording stops future automatic posts but does not cancel requests already in progress. Disconnecting Studyplus removes local authentication credentials but does not delete your in-app study history or Studyplus delivery history, revoke authorization on Studyplus's side, or remove previously posted Studyplus records. Deleting a saved page does not delete exported backups or files at a sharing destination. Make any needed backup before deleting the app. Device backups provided by iOS or cloud providers follow their own settings and policies.

Information you send for support

If you email support, the email address, message, and attachments you provide are used to respond and investigate issues. Do not send unnecessary personal information or study materials.

Contact and changes

Contact support@toybird.com. We will update this page when features or information-handling practices change.
