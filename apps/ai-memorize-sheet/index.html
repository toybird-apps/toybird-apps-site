AI Study Sheet

Turn textbooks, worksheets, notes, photos, and PDFs into memory tests. AI finds key terms and creates masks on your device, then you can refine them, add your own, and tap to reveal the answer.

What’s new in v3.1.1

More reliable mask positioning

Mask geometry is now corrected more defensively when visual elements such as underlines interfere with OCR positioning, helping masks stay aligned with the recognized text.

Keep the answer location visible

With “Keep mask position after reveal” enabled, revealed masks remain visible as thin outlines for both individual reveal and Show All. Turn it off if you prefer the revealed area to disappear completely.

Draw manual masks more naturally

In Set Masks Manually, drag horizontally across the exact area you want to hide. Vertical dragging moves horizontally written materials, including PDFs, so you can navigate without accidentally creating a mask.

Return to recent materials faster

Recently opened materials are easier to reach from Home, so you can continue studying without first searching through the library.

Build your study sheet with AI, then finish it yourself

AI selects key terms

Import a photo, camera capture, or PDF. Standard mode recognizes text and selects useful terms to mask. You can choose Fewer, Standard, More, or Before a test depending on how much you want hidden.

Adjust AI Selection

Keep the AI-generated masks as a starting point, then add or remove candidates to finish the sheet the way you want. You do not need to rebuild the page from scratch.

Set Masks Manually

If you prefer full control, start with no active masks and draw the areas yourself. OCR candidates remain available underneath, and you can return to the AI selection when needed.

Pre-marked materials

A dedicated mode detects materials that already contain red or orange text, highlighting, or similar markings. It uses those existing visual cues and does not restore text that is fully hidden in the source image.

Study, review, share, and organize

Study while long imports continue

PDF pages are processed in order, so you can start with pages that are ready. Pause, resume, or retry an import when needed. Free save slots, device storage, and document size still apply.

Flexible review modes

Study in order, randomly, or with weak points first. Choose 10, 20, 50, or all questions, resume an unfinished session, and use folders or search to narrow the material.

Share study materials

Send study materials in AI Study Sheet format from the paid version. Recipients can receive shared materials even on the free version, subject to their available save slots.

Search, organize, and back up

Search titles, notes, recognized text, and terms. Organize materials with folders and back up pages, images, masks, and study history together.

Free saves grow from 3 to 10

Start with three free saved pages. Complete your first study session to permanently expand the free limit to 10. Reserved slots and active drafts also affect available capacity.

One purchase for unlimited saves

Unlimited Saves removes the saved-page limit with a one-time purchase and no subscription. Check the price displayed in the App Store before purchase in your region.

Stronger key-term detection

Key-term detection has been expanded across Japanese, English, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Portuguese to better support different study subjects.

10 interface languages

The interface supports English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese.

Your materials are processed on device

Material images, recognized text, key terms, masks, and saved data are processed on your device. Study materials are not uploaded to external AI services or Toybird Labs servers. Exporting a backup or sharing sends data only to the destination you choose.

Privacy Policy

System requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 16 or later. iOS / iPadOS 26 or later can use newer document recognition. Background processing is used only on supported devices when permitted by the OS. Otherwise, keep the app open or return to resume processing.
