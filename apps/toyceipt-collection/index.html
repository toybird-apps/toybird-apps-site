Toyceipt Collection

Scan an everyday receipt. Meet a new Pal. Build your collection.
Each receipt becomes a small surprise with its own look, rarity, family, attribute, personality, and signatures.

SupportPrivacy Policy

Scan → Reveal → Collect

Take a photo of a receipt or choose one from your photo library. Toyceipt reads it on device, reveals a Pal, and adds that character to your collection.

Meet different Pals

Sixteen body families combine with palettes, eyes, expressions, markings, accessories, attributes, personalities, and signatures so each discovery can feel different.

Keep discovering

Use the Discovery Book, weekly discoveries, achievements, and the Game Center Discovery Score to track new traits instead of competing on how much you spend.

Built as a game, with receipt privacy in mind

Receipt images are processed on your device and are not stored by Toyceipt Collection. Readable purchase details are structured and kept locally in this version; they are not uploaded to Toybird Labs servers. The app does not request device GPS location.

Toyceipt Collection is free and uses a small Google AdMob banner. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used for advertising privacy choices where required. Optional game analytics can be controlled in the app.
