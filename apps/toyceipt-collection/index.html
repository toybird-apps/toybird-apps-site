Scan. Reveal. Meet your Pal.

Take a photo of a receipt or choose one from your library. A few seconds later, its Pal appears and joins your collection.

Every Pal has its own character

Families, colors, patterns, accessories, attributes, personalities, signatures, and rarity combine to make each new arrival feel different.

Your everyday collection keeps unfolding

Fill the Discovery Book, find new traits each week, unlock achievements, and watch your Discovery Score grow with every new find.

Your receipt becomes a Pal, then stays private

Receipt recognition happens on your iPhone. The image is discarded after processing, while readable purchase details are kept locally on your device in this version. Device GPS is not used for receipt processing.

Toyceipt Collection is free and uses a small Google AdMob banner. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used for advertising privacy choices where required. Optional game analytics can be controlled in the app.
