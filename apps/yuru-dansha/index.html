Support Privacy Policy

Today’s 10

Open one short daily session built around rediscovery. When possible, On This Day uses photos from the same month and day in past years, with old-photo or random fallbacks when needed.

Mark photos to See Again

Save an app-local See Again mark without changing Apple Photos Favorites, revisit marked photos later, and share a rediscovered still photo with the standard iOS share sheet.

Rediscover your way

Choose a year and month, browse older or random photos, review screenshots or videos, and keep the existing Keep / Let Go flow with confirmation before deletion.

Designed for gentle, private rediscovery

Original photos and videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to Toybird Labs servers. No account registration is required. Optional daily reminders can open Today’s 10 directly.

Yuru Dansha uses Google AdMob for banner and native advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent handling where required.
