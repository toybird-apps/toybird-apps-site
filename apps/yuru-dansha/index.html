A small daily photo ritual

Revisit ten photos without sorting your whole library

A photo cleanup does not have to start with deleting thousands of pictures. Yuru Dansha begins with a small set to look at. The point is to enjoy revisiting memories, with optional organizing decisions along the way.

Today's 10

Open a short daily selection instead of browsing an entire camera roll. The app can draw from On This Day memories, older photos, or other available selections.

On This Day

See memories taken on the same date in previous years when available. If the library has none for that date, the app can fall back to other older or random photos.

See Again

Mark a photo that deserves another look. This is the app's own marker, separate from Apple Photos Favorites, so you can revisit it without changing the system Favorites list.

Optional cleanup

If a photo no longer needs to stay, use the existing Keep / Let Go flow. Deletion requires confirmation; reviewing a photo alone never deletes it.

An easy session to try

  1. Open Today's 10 and look through the day's small set.
  2. Pause at an On This Day photo to revisit an earlier moment.
  3. Mark meaningful pictures as See Again or share a selected still photo.
  4. Leave cleanup for another day, or choose a confirmed action for a photo you no longer want.

Your original photos are processed on device; optional ads and consent handling are described in the privacy policy. Read support for more on photo permissions and the review flow.

Designed for gentle, private rediscovery

Original photos and videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to Toybird Labs servers. No account registration is required. Optional daily reminders can open Today’s 10 directly.
