macOS utility for Web Gmail users

Gmail Spotlight Indexer

Search selected Gmail metadata from the standard macOS Spotlight window without setting up Apple Mail or downloading full message bodies.

View Support Read Privacy Policy

What it does

Gmail results in Spotlight

After you add a Gmail account, the app registers Gmail-derived search items with Core Spotlight so they can appear in the normal macOS Spotlight search experience.

Designed for Web Gmail

Gmail Spotlight Indexer is for people who use Gmail in a browser and do not want to set up Apple Mail just to find old messages from Spotlight.

Open results in Gmail

When you choose an indexed result, the app opens the matching Gmail conversation in your default browser using the signed-in Google account.

Metadata-first indexing

Gmail Spotlight Indexer is intentionally limited to message metadata. It uses Gmail metadata access to read information such as subject, sender, recipients, date, labels, unread/starred/important state, attachment presence, message ID, and thread ID.

The app does not fetch full Gmail message bodies, does not download attachment files, and does not request permission to send, delete, or modify your email.

Snippets are optional per Gmail account and are off by default. If enabled, Gmail’s short snippet may be included in the local Spotlight index for that account. You can turn snippets off again and rebuild the index without snippets.

Account-level controls

Multiple Gmail accounts

Add more than one Gmail account and manage each mailbox separately.

Choose history range

For each account, choose how far back to keep indexed: recent 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or all mail.

Pause, resume, remove

Pause a long-running sync, resume it later, or remove an account. Removing an account deletes its local metadata, saved tokens, and Spotlight index items.

How it works

  1. Open Gmail Spotlight Indexer on your Mac.
  2. Click Add Gmail Account.
  3. Sign in on Google’s official sign-in page in your default browser.
  4. Allow Gmail metadata access.
  5. The app indexes the recent 30 days first so results can become searchable quickly.
  6. Depending on your account settings, older mail is indexed afterward while the app is open.

Gmail Spotlight Indexer stores Gmail-derived metadata locally on your Mac. It does not send Gmail metadata to Toybird Labs servers or third-party analytics services.

System Requirements

Gmail Spotlight Indexer requires macOS 13 or later and a Google account with Gmail access. A browser session signed in to the target Google account is recommended when opening Gmail results.
