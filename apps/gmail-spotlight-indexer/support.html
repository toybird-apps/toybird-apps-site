Basic Setup

  1. Open Gmail Spotlight Indexer on your Mac.
  2. Click Add Gmail Account.
  3. Sign in on Google’s official sign-in page in your default browser.
  4. Approve the requested Gmail metadata permission.
  5. Wait for the recent 30-day index to complete.
  6. Open macOS Spotlight and search for a subject, sender, recipient, label, or other indexed metadata.

Account Settings

Settings are managed separately for each Gmail account.

  • Index mail history: choose recent 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or all mail.
  • Include Gmail snippets: optional and off by default. When enabled, Gmail’s short snippet may be included in Spotlight search for that account.
  • Pause Sync: pause long-running indexing, especially when indexing all mail.
  • Remove Account: delete the account’s local metadata, saved tokens, and Spotlight index items from this Mac.

Troubleshooting

Spotlight results do not appear

Wait until the recent 30-day sync is complete, then try searching again. If results still do not appear, use Rebuild Spotlight Index in the app and allow macOS time to refresh Spotlight results.

The result opens Gmail but shows the wrong account

Gmail results open in your default browser using the account email. Make sure the same Google account is signed in to Gmail in that browser.

Sync takes a long time

Large mailboxes can take time, especially when Index mail history is set to All mail. You can pause sync, narrow the history range, and resume later.

Google sign-in does not complete

Make sure you finish sign-in and permission approval on Google’s page. During testing or staged rollout, only approved test accounts may be able to complete Google sign-in.

I removed an account. Does it delete email from Gmail?

No. Removing an account from Gmail Spotlight Indexer only deletes local app metadata, Keychain tokens, and Spotlight index items on your Mac. It does not delete or change your Gmail messages.

Privacy and Data Handling

Gmail Spotlight Indexer does not fetch full Gmail message bodies, does not download attachment files, and does not request permission to send, delete, or modify Gmail messages.
