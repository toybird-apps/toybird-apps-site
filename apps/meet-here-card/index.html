Meet Here Card

Show the last few steps a map cannot.
Combine photos, arrows, a map, a QR code, and step-by-step route images into one clear meeting-point card.

Support Privacy Policy

Point to the exact spot

Take a photo or choose one from Photos, then add arrows, circles, editable text, cropping, and mosaic so the other person knows exactly where to look.

Add a map and QR code

Search for a station, facility, or address with Apple Maps, fine-tune the pin, and include a map image, Apple Maps link, and QR code in the finished card.

Explain the route step by step

Combine the main photo with up to three additional photos. Add direction icons and short instructions to create a vertical route card with as many as four steps.

Use on-device assistance

Detect faces for quick mosaic placement and recognize text from signs. Recognized text can be reused as an annotation, title, landmark, or note.

Start from the Photos share sheet

Send one selected photo to Meet Here Card from the standard iOS share sheet, then continue editing it in the app.

Save and reuse familiar places

Add a date, time, landmark, and a situation-based template. Save cards in History or Favorites and update them later without starting over.

Designed for privacy

Photo editing, face detection, text recognition, card rendering, and QR-code generation are performed on your iPhone. Saved cards remain in the app’s private local storage, and a photo received through the share extension is held temporarily in local shared storage until it is imported.

No account is required. Meet Here Card does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not upload your photos or meeting details to a developer server. Place search and map imagery use Apple Maps services only when you use map features.
