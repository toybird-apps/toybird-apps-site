AI LifeMatch

Support

Help and frequently asked questions for AI LifeMatch.

Getting Started

Create your profile and answer the first 24 questions. You can then invite your partner with a QR code, let your partner answer on the same device, or import an updated profile from the same partner. If you want more detail, continue from the existing 24 answers to the 48-question diagnosis.

What the Results Mean

LifeMatch shows your pair as one of 12 LifeMatch types and compares themes such as communication, living rhythm, money, future planning, and relationship balance. Results are designed for self-reflection and conversation, not as a definitive judgment about a relationship.

Detailed Report and On-Device AI

On supported devices, Apple’s on-device AI can create explanations using only the 12 numeric tendency scores. Names and individual question answers are not sent to AI. If AI generation is unavailable, the app uses on-device diagnostic logic. A rewarded ad may be required before opening the detailed report.

Partner Invitations, QR Codes, and Share Cards

You can share an invitation that includes a QR code. The recipient can scan it with the camera or choose a saved QR image, and the app can recognize later updates from the same shared profile. You can also create result share cards through the standard iOS share sheet. Shared profiles exclude individual question answers.

Deleting Data

You can delete registered people, compatibility results, and all app data from within the app. Deleted compatibility results can be restored from Settings when the restore option is available.

Advertising

AI LifeMatch uses Google AdMob for banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads. Ads may not appear immediately after a new install, in some regions, or when no ad is available.
