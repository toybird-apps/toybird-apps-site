Understand English conversations as they happen.

Features

Works with your meeting tools

Translate supported Mac audio from Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, training sessions, videos and webinars. Microphone input is available where supported.

One shortcut: Option + S

Start and stop immediately. The shortcut can be customized in settings. No meeting-specific plugin is required.

Singapore English recognition adaptation

Custom acoustic machine learning evaluates Singapore English characteristics. With sufficient evidence and system support, recognition may adapt to en-SG; general English remains the default.

Focused on understanding now

KoeSub prioritizes timely comprehension over a perfect transcript. Post-meeting automatic summaries are not included.

Compatibility and limitations

macOS 15 or later · English speech → Japanese subtitles. Audio capture and speech recognition permissions may be required. Caption timing, speech recognition, and translation quality depend on device settings, accents, background noise, and available Apple language resources. This is an understanding aid, not guaranteed verbatim transcription or professional interpretation.

Support and privacy

KoeSub does not upload audio, transcripts or translations to Toybird Labs servers. Apple system speech recognition and translation frameworks are used. KoeSub does not provide post-meeting summaries.
