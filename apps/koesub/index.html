macOS live translated subtitles

KoeSub

KoeSub is a simple macOS menu bar app that listens to selected audio sources and displays translated subtitles in real time.

Read SupportRead Privacy Policy

Translate Mac audio

Use KoeSub to display translated subtitles for audio playing on your Mac, such as meetings, videos, and other spoken content.

Use microphone input

Choose microphone input when you want spoken words around you to be recognized and translated into the selected target language.

Menu bar control

Start and stop translation from the menu bar. KoeSub stays idle until you explicitly turn translation on.

Listening workflow

Follow spoken English with Japanese subtitles on your Mac

KoeSub is designed for situations where it is more useful to follow the conversation as it happens than to wait for a transcript after the fact. Select a supported Mac audio source or microphone input, turn translation on, and read subtitles during playback or a conversation.

Remote discussions

When a meeting includes spoken English, subtitles can provide Japanese-language assistance while the discussion is still in progress. Audio availability depends on your Mac's permissions and the selected source.

Videos and presentations

Use translated subtitles to keep up with English explanations in video content or product demos on your Mac, without first exporting the recording to a separate translation workflow.

Microphone-based listening

For speech around your computer, choose microphone input where supported. Your environment, microphone, and background noise affect recognition accuracy.

From sound to subtitle

  1. Choose a supported audio source or microphone in the app.
  2. Configure the target translation language, including Japanese where available.
  3. Enable subtitles and read the translated text while audio continues.
  4. Turn subtitles off when you no longer need them; KoeSub stays idle until activated again.

Subtitles can be delayed, abbreviated, or imperfect. KoeSub is assistance for understanding, not a guaranteed verbatim record or professional interpreter. See supported system settings and help.

Built for quick understanding

KoeSub is designed for situations where a real-time translated subtitle can help you follow spoken content more easily.

Speech recognition and translation quality can vary depending on audio clarity, speaker accent, background noise, device settings, supported language availability, and Apple system framework behavior. KoeSub is not intended to guarantee perfect transcription or translation.
