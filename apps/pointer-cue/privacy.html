Pointer Cue

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Pointer Cue respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data Collection

Pointer Cue does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

Local Input and Window State

To display its cues, Pointer Cue processes active-app, visible-window position, Command-key, modifier, and mouse-button state locally on your Mac. Input Monitoring is used for the Command-key cursor cue and the configured Range Selection gesture.

Focus Cue uses local macOS window and application state to identify the active top-level window and place the visual mask around it.

Pointer Cue does not record, store, transmit, or analyze typed text, passwords, keyboard history, or mouse history.

Screen Content

Pointer Cue does not require Screen Recording permission and does not capture, upload, or transmit your screen content. Its visual overlays are generated locally on your Mac.

Local Settings

Preferences such as selected color, display language, and launch-at-login settings are stored locally on your Mac.

External Communication and Third-Party SDKs

Pointer Cue does not connect to external servers and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact support@toybird.com.
