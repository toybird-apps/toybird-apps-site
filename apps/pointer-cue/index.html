macOS menu bar utility

Pointer Cue

Show exactly where to look.
Pointer Cue v1.3.0 is a free Mac menu bar utility for demos, presentations, tutorials, and screen sharing. Show the pointer and clicks, focus the active window, or frame an area without entering a drawing mode. New users can try every Pro feature for 60 days.

Pointer Cue

Press and release Command to display a temporary ring around the pointer and draw attention to the right spot.

Click Cue

A visual spark appears after a completed left click so viewers can follow exactly where an action happened. Drags do not trigger Click Cue.

Focus Cue

Keep the active top-level window clear while a translucent mask reduces visual distraction across the usable area of that display.

Range Cue — Pro

Choose Command + Drag, Control + Command + Drag, or Option + Command + Drag to frame an important area temporarily. Range Cue is included during the 60-day Pro Preview and with Pointer Cue Pro.

Multi-display aware

Focus Cue masks only the display containing the active window. Other displays stay clear for notes, scripts, meeting controls, or other working material.

Follows normal Mac workflows

Focus Cue follows Command + Tab, window cycling, clicks, Dock, Stage Manager, Mission Control, Finder Quick Look, and app-driven activation.

Free and Pro colors

White and red remain free. Orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, and custom colors are included during the Pro Preview and with Pointer Cue Pro.

Local and non-blocking

The visual overlays do not block normal Mac interaction. Pointer Cue does not require Screen Recording permission or capture your screen content.

Free and Pointer Cue Pro

Pointer Cue is free to download from the Mac App Store. New users receive the complete Pro feature set for 60 days from their first acquisition.

After the preview, Pointer Cue, Click Cue, Focus Cue, white, and red remain free. Pointer Cue Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks Range Cue, its shortcut options, additional preset colors, and custom colors.

Customers who purchased Pointer Cue before v1.3.0 receive Lifetime Pro at no additional cost.

Windows version

The existing Windows version remains available from Microsoft Store, but active feature development is currently paused. Newer macOS features and the Free / Pro model may not be available on Windows.

System Requirements

macOS 13.5 or later. Designed for both Intel Macs and Apple silicon Macs.

Pointer Cue processes active-app, visible-window position, Command-key, modifier, and mouse-button state locally to display its cues. It does not capture screen content or record, store, transmit, or analyze typed text, passwords, or input history.
