macOS menu bar utility

Pointer Cue

Show exactly where to look.
Guide attention during demos, screen sharing, training, and remote meetings with a cursor ring, active-window Focus Cue, and quick range highlights.

Support Privacy Policy

Cursor Ring

Press and release the Command key to display a temporary shrinking ring around the pointer and draw attention to the right spot.

Focus Cue

Keep the active top-level window clear while a 50% translucent white mask reduces visual distraction across the usable area of that display.

Range Selection

Choose Command + Drag, Control + Command + Drag, Option + Command + Drag, or Off, then drag to outline an important area with a temporary fading rectangle.

Multi-display aware

Focus Cue masks only the display containing the active window. Other displays stay completely clear for notes, scripts, meeting controls, or other working material.

Follows normal Mac workflows

Focus Cue follows Command + Tab, window cycling, clicks, Dock, Stage Manager, Mission Control, Finder Quick Look, and app-driven activation.

Colors, languages, and login

Choose preset or custom cue colors, select a display language or follow macOS, and optionally launch Pointer Cue automatically when you sign in.

Local and non-blocking

The overlays ignore mouse events, the menu bar and Dock remain unobstructed, and Pointer Cue does not require Screen Recording permission or capture your screen content.

System Requirements

macOS 13.5 or later. Designed for both Intel Macs and Apple silicon Macs.

Pointer Cue processes active-app, visible-window position, Command-key, modifier, and mouse-button state locally to display its cues. It does not capture screen content or record, store, transmit, or analyze typed text, passwords, or input history.
