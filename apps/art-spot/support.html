Basic Usage

  1. Choose normal play, Today’s Gallery, Weekly Exhibition, or My Gallery from Home.
  2. In normal play, select Easy, Normal, or Hard and choose an artwork.
  3. Tap the area that differs from the original artwork. Pinch to zoom and pan when needed.
  4. Use a hint or compare with the original artwork if you need help.
  5. Optional daily reminders can be enabled from Settings after notification permission is granted.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many puzzles are included?

The current version includes 180 artworks and 540 stages across Easy, Normal, and Hard difficulties.

What are Today’s Gallery and Weekly Exhibition?

Today’s Gallery offers three daily stages, while Weekly Exhibition offers seven stages for the current week. Their progress is tracked separately from normal play.

Can I zoom into the artwork?

Yes. Pinch to zoom and pan while solving a puzzle, then reset the view as needed.

Does the app have a time limit?

No. You can look carefully and solve each puzzle at your own pace. Best times are saved locally on your device.

Why do ads appear?

Art Spot is a free app supported by interstitial advertising. Ads are shown at natural breaks in gameplay. If you want to review privacy choices, open Settings in the app when that option is available.

Can I report an inappropriate or irrelevant ad?

Please send a screenshot and the approximate time the ad appeared to support@toybird.com.
